# Pages

A quick tour of every page in the app, what it shows and where its options live.

- [Library](/pages/library/), home, tracks, albums, artists, folders and more
- [YouTube](/pages/youtube/), feed, playlists, channels, history and downloads
- [Other](/pages/other/), stats, sync manager and about

::: callout tip
You control which pages appear as tabs and their order, see [Library Tabs](/settings/6-extras-settings/#library-tabs).
:::
