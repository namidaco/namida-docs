# YouTube Pages

The pages of the [YouTube](/features/youtube/) section.
Most pages have local variant and hosted variant for when you are signed in.

### Home {#home}

Your YouTube feed, with notifications and subscriptions when [signed in](/settings/5-youtube-settings/#accounts).

### Playlists {#playlists}

[`⚙️ Playlists & History ↗`](/features/playlists-history/)

Your YouTube playlists, along with the built-in ones: History, Most Played and Liked. You can create playlists, and import your existing playlists and channels from a YouTube takeout export.

### Channels {#channels}

Your subscribed channels, they can be organized into groups. Each channel page shows its videos and info.

### History {#history}

Your YouTube watch history inside Namida, separate from the local one, with its own Most Played.

### Downloads {#downloads}

[`⚙️ Downloads ↗`](/features/youtube/#downloads)

Manage all download tasks, pause, resume, retry or cancel, with a failed only filter. Playlists get their own batch download page.

### Search {#search}

Search YouTube directly, results include videos, playlists and channels. Offline search is available for browsing what you already have.
