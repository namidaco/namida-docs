# Membership

Namida is free, with no ads and no tracking. Nearly everything in these docs works without paying anything, and without an account.

A membership is how you support the project and keep it growing. As a thank you, it unlocks a few extras on top, mostly for YouTube while signed in to your account.

### What It Unlocks {#benefits}

| Feature                                                  | Needs                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Like, dislike & mark videos as watched                   | Free, just sign in                                  |
| Your playlists: browse, create, add & remove videos      | Free, just sign in                                  |
| Subscriptions & notifications                            | Free, just sign in                                  |
| Home feed & watch history                                | `cutie`                                             |
| Comments, subscribing & channel notifications            | `cutie`                                             |
| Save to library, edit & delete playlists                 | `cutie`                                             |
| Hide or turn off notifications                           | `cutie`                                             |
| Create a listening party on the Namida server            | `cutie`                                             |
| Party Mode & Crossfade                                   | `cutie`, or find the [easter egg](/tips/#misc-tips) |
| Multiple YouTube accounts                                | `pookie`                                            |

::: callout tip
To keep a public playlist for free, use "Import playlist" from its menu instead, it's saved inside Namida. [`🎉 YouTube Playlists ↗`](/features/youtube/#playlists)
:::

### Free for Everyone {#free}

Everything else is free, here is a taste of it.

**YouTube, no account needed** [`🎉 YouTube feature ↗`](/features/youtube/)

- Streaming in the best quality, audio only mode, data saver and radio mixes.
- Downloads of any video, audio or whole playlist, with tags, filename formats, splitting by chapters, removing sponsor segments and multiple connections.
- Caching for offline playback, with a priority system that keeps what matters to you.
- The full video player, with gestures, fullscreen, subtitles, chapters and picture-in-picture.
- Search with filters, and offline search through everything you watched before.
- Every option in the video menu: favourites, add to playlist, download, cache, copy, go to channel, play next, play after, play last, stop after this video and more.
- Your own local YouTube playlists, favourites, subscriptions with groups, history and most played.
- SponsorBlock, Return YouTube Dislike, comments, related videos, hashtags and channel pages.
- Info of private and deleted videos, thanks to Filmot.
- Importing your history, playlists and subscriptions from a YouTube takeout.

**YouTube, signed in** [`⚙️ Configure Accounts ↗`](/settings/5-youtube-settings/#accounts)

- Like and dislike videos, your votes can go to Return YouTube Dislike too.
- Your account playlists, browse and sort them, create new ones, and add or remove videos. The add to playlist sheet shows which ones already have the video.
- Your subscriptions list, with all the latest videos from your channels.
- Your notifications, play all unread videos at once, mark them as read, and open comment notifications with their replies.
- Mark videos as watched on your account.
- Personalized related videos and mixes.
- Better download speed, and fixes for some playback issues.

**Everything else**

- A folder based [library](/features/library-indexing/) with a powerful indexer, smart sorting and [media servers](/features/media-servers/) with offline caching.
- [Playback](/features/playback/) with a reliable queue system, sound control, equalizer, replay gain, video integration and lyrics with romanization.
- [Playlists and history](/features/playlists-history/), M3U and smart playlists, most played, stats and your year.
- A full [tag editor](/features/tag-editor/), for single and multiple tracks.
- [Sync](/features/sync/) between your devices, and [listening parties](/features/party/), joining them or hosting on your own device or server.
- [Home screen widget](/features/home-widget/), [system integration](/features/system-integration/), [shortcuts](/features/shortcuts/) and a mini lyrics window.
- Themes and [customizations](/settings/4-customization-settings/) for almost every part of the app.

### Getting One {#get}

Open the membership page from Settings -> Youtube -> Manage Your Accounts, then press the membership tile. It shows your current tier and what it unlocks. The same page opens from the "Manage" button when something needs a membership.

There are two ways to get one:

- **Patreon**, subscribe on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/namidaco), then press "Sign in to your patreon account" in the membership page. If the browser doesn't bring you back to Namida, press "Issues?" and paste the link it ended on.
- **Coupon**, donate through [kofi](https://ko-fi.com/namidaco) or [buymeacoffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/namidaco) and you get a coupon code by email, which can take a few days. Enter the code and your email in the Coupon section, then press Claim. The page shows when it expires.

::: callout info
YouTube features still need you to sign in to your YouTube account, the membership only unlocks them. [`⚙️ Configure Accounts ↗`](/settings/5-youtube-settings/#accounts)
:::

More about the membership in [this post](https://www.patreon.com/posts/namida-yt-112913142).

Every membership, big or small, helps Namida stay free for everyone. Thank you for being part of it.
