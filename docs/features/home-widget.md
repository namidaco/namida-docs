# Home Screen Widget

`💻 Android only`

A resizable player widget for your home screen, with a full settings screen behind it.

### Adding It {#adding}

Long press your home screen, open the widgets list and pick Namida. Drop it anywhere and resize it, the layout adapts to the size you give it.

### Configuring It {#configuring}

The settings screen opens right after placing the widget, with a live preview of your changes on top. You can open it again later from [`⚙️ Home Screen Widget ↗`](/settings/4-customization-settings/#home-widget), or from your launcher if it supports reconfiguring widgets.

::: callout tip
Opening it from inside Namida applies your changes to every widget you already placed, and to new ones. Opening it from the launcher only changes that one widget, so you can have two widgets with different looks.
:::

#### Appearance {#appearance}

- Theme, System, Light or Dark.
- Colors from artwork, the widget follows the current track colors.
- Background, Solid, Gradient or Blurred art.
- Artwork effect, None, Glow or Shadow.
- Background opacity, corner radius and artwork rounding.

#### Layout {#layout}

- Arrangement, Auto, Row, Card or Compact. Auto picks the one that fits the current size.
- Content size and artwork size.
- Show or hide the artwork, the title and the subtitle.

#### Controls {#controls}

Choose which buttons appear: Favourite, Shuffle, Previous, Play / Pause, Next, Repeat mode and Stop.

Shuffle and Repeat mode show their current state, they look dimmed while they are off. [`🎉 Queue System ↗`](/features/playback/#queue)

#### Tap Actions {#tap-actions}

Set what tapping the widget does, and what tapping the artwork does. Both can Open app, Play / Pause, or do nothing.

::: callout info
Press Reset at the bottom to go back to the default look.
:::
